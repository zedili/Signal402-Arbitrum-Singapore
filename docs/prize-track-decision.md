# Signal402 prize-track decision brief

Status: **pre-event planning only; no track has been selected.**

This brief records the public prize structure observed on Sep 7, 2026 and
reduces the final track choice to an explicit owner decision. Re-check the live
submission form and Terms before selecting anything.

## Public prize structure

| Category | Public amount | Public split | Signal402 fit | Material condition |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| Overall Prize | 70,000 USDC | 40,000 / 20,000 / 10,000 | Strong if the final build proves the complete paid-agent workflow, contract quality, and in-window engineering | Terms describe staged 25% / 25% / 50% payment, a grant agreement, an Arbitrum-only check-in period, mainnet launch, and agreed KPIs |
| Promising Products Track | 15,000 USDC | 7,000 / 5,000 / 3,000 | Strong for a testnet MVP with a clear buyer, transparent unit price, and credible validation roadmap | The Terms appear to exclude the “Best Promising Products” category from the staged payment conditions; verify the final wording before relying on that exception |
| Grants | 30,000 USDC pool | Case by case | Plausible only with measurable follow-on milestones | Entirely discretionary; selection and amount are not guaranteed, and any agreement requires separate owner review |

The event page applies the same four judging criteria to Overall and Promising
Products: smart-contract quality, product-market fit, innovation and
creativity, and real problem solving. For each three-prize set, at least one
award is reserved for a Robinhood Chain project and at least one for an
Arbitrum project. Signal402's verified Arbitrum Sepolia deployment qualifies as
an Arbitrum-chain project; it does not qualify as a Robinhood Chain deployment.

## Recommended selection rule

1. If the form permits multiple honest selections, select **Overall Prize** and
   **Promising Products Track**. Treat any grant consideration as a separate
   post-award decision, not advance acceptance of milestones.
2. If the form permits only one selection and the owner does not want potential
   Arbitrum exclusivity, mainnet-launch, KPI, or grant-agreement obligations,
   choose **Promising Products Track** after confirming its exception in the
   then-current Terms.
3. If the form permits only one selection and the owner is willing to review a
   higher-value milestone agreement if Signal402 wins, choose **Overall
   Prize**. Selection does not authorize signing the later agreement.
4. Do not select or imply a Robinhood Chain deployment unless a real deployment
   is completed and the owner separately approves that strategy.

## Decision gates

Before the final form is saved or submitted, show the owner:

- whether the form allows one or multiple categories;
- the exact category labels and any category-specific declarations;
- the current Terms language governing the Promising Products exception;
- any checkbox that authorizes grant consideration or milestone obligations;
- the final recommendation based on the verified in-window build.

Track selection, grant consideration, and any later prize agreement remain
separate owner-confirmation gates.
